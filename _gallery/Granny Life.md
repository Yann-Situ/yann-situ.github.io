I made two granny squares for the project [Granny Life](https://www.grannylifecrochet.com/) initiated by Laura Taalman.
In total, 242 unique granny squares have been combined in a two-sided 11x11 blanket.
Every pattern was generated by a cellular automaton.
The whole piece was exposed in [Maison Poincaré](https://www.ihp.fr/fr/musee-maison-poincare) for the exhibition [Création, entre arts et mathematiques](https://www.ihp.fr/en/actualites-science-et-societe/creation-entre-art-et-mathematiques-aap) in 2026.
