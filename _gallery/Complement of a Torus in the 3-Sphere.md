Here is a representation of a continuous transformation from the complement of a torus in a 3-sphere, to two intertwined annulus.
This is an example to help understand Alexander duality.

The 3-sphere is represented with the Gott projection. In this projection, the 3-sphere is divided in two hemispheres (north and south), which are virtually glued together on their boundary. This projection can also be used for the 2-sphere, as shown below with the Gott projection of the earth.
