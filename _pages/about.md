Hello, I'm Yann-Situ, I like maths and many other creative stuff.

I am currently finishing a PhD in theoretical computer science under the supervision of [Alexandra Bac](https://pageperso.lis-lab.fr/alexandra.bac/) and [Aldo Gonzalez-Lorenzo](https://pageperso.lis-lab.fr/aldo.gonzalez-lorenzo/), in the [GMOD team](https://g-mod.lis-lab.fr/), at the [LIS](https://www.lis-lab.fr/), in [Aix-Marseille Université](https://www.univ-amu.fr/fr).
My expertise of research is computational topology (especially combinatorial homology) and discrete geometry.
More broadly, I love mathematics fields that have a strong visual aspect.
